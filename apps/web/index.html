FAQ

Questions owners ask first

Which messengers work today?

Telegram works directly: you connect your own bot with a token from @BotFather. WhatsApp and Instagram messages come in through a secure webhook, so you connect them with a bridge such as Make or n8n, or through a WhatsApp Business API provider. Replies go back through an outgoing webhook.

Which AI writes the replies?

Claude by Anthropic. It is included, and you can connect your own Anthropic API key instead. If Claude is not available, simple built-in rules answer, and you can always take over the chat.

What happens when the AI does not know the answer?

It is told to answer only from your services, schedule and knowledge base. When it is not sure, it hands the chat to a person, and the chat appears under "Needs attention" on your dashboard.

Does it work in my country and currency?

Each workspace has its own currency and time zone, and bookings and reminders follow your local clock. The AI answers in the languages you list in your business settings.

How much does it cost?

Creating an account is free and needs no credit card.

Where is my data stored and who sees it?

Your data is kept in the VoroniX database and is used only to run the service for you. The Privacy Policy lists every service that receives data and why.
