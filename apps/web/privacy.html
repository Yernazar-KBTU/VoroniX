1. Who we are

VoroniX is an online CRM with an AI administrator for businesses that work by appointment. It is operated by [legal name of the operator], [registered address] ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [contact email].

2. Two kinds of data

  • Account data is about you, the person who signs up and uses VoroniX. We decide how it is used, as described here.
  • Workspace data is what you and your AI administrator keep about your own business and clients: names, phone numbers, messages, bookings and payments. You decide what to collect and why. We process it on your behalf and only to run VoroniX for you.

If you are a client of a business that uses VoroniX, that business controls your data. Please contact it first; we will help it answer you.

3. What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name and email address; your business name, type of business, country, time zone and currency.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrypt hash. We never store or see it in readable form.
  • If you use "Continue with Google": your Google account ID, name and email address, and whether Google has verified that email.

When you sign in

  • A random session token. We store only its SHA-256 hash, together with when the session started and ends and the user agent text your browser sends.
  • The time of your last sign-in.
  • If you ask to reset your password: a one-time token (stored only as a hash) and when it was created, when it expires and when it was used.

While you use VoroniX

  • Workspace data: clients (names, phone numbers, messenger user IDs, notes), services, staff and their hours, bookings, payment records, conversations with your clients including the replies written by the AI, knowledge base entries and business settings.
  • Integration settings, including tokens and keys you enter. After you save them, only their last four characters are shown back.
  • A log of AI replies: which engine answered, how long it took, which actions it used, how many tokens it processed, and errors.
  • Server logs: for each request, the time, method, address requested (with secret values removed), IP address, browser user agent and the response status.

4. How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep your workspace separate from every other business.
  • To run the AI administrator: answer your clients, book and move visits, and send reminders.
  • To send service emails, such as password reset links. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.
  • To keep VoroniX secure and working: limiting failed sign-in attempts, preventing abuse and fixing problems.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your workspace data to advertise to you or to your clients, and we do not use it to train AI models.

  • Server logs: [how long logs are kept].

8. Security

  • Every business has its own separate space (schema) in the database.
  • Passwords are stored only as scrypt hashes; session and reset tokens only as SHA-256 hashes.
  • Failed sign-ins are limited to 10 attempts in 15 minutes.
  • Session cookies are HttpOnly and sent only over HTTPS.
  • The AI cannot change your data directly. It can only use a fixed set of actions: find free times, link a client card, book, look up, move or cancel a visit, create a payment link and hand the chat to a person.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

You can see and change most of your data inside VoroniX. To get a copy of your data or to delete your account, write to us at the address in section 1. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights, such as access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. We will answer within the time the law sets.
